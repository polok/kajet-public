Everything for school and university, in one app

TODAY

Your whole day, grades and AI tips

  • "This week" rings: your weekly average with an up or down arrow, and how many assignments and tests are behind you.
  • Your day as a timeline: lessons in subject colors, breaks and free periods, the lesson in progress and the minutes left in it.
  • "To do": the nearest test with a countdown, overdue homework and today's tasks that you check off with one tap.
  • Quick add for a grade, an assignment, a test or a study session.

TIMETABLE

Your timetable, always at hand

  • Day and week views, A and B weeks, rooms and times.
  • Cancelled lessons, substitutions and room changes for a single day.
  • After-school events: sports, music, tutoring, clubs, with a reminder of when to leave.
  • A Home Screen widget and a Live Activity counting down the lesson in progress.

GRADES AND AVERAGES

Your average in every subject

  • A simple or weighted average with your own categories (quiz, test, oral answer…), comments and retakes.
  • The average for each subject and overall. Leave out subjects that don't count.
  • Statistics: average over time, your best subject and the one pulling you down.

HOMEWORK AND TESTS

No homework will catch you off guard

  • Assignments, tests and quizzes grouped into today, tomorrow, this week and later.
  • Reminders a day or three days ahead, in the evening or at a time you choose.
  • Turn a finished test or assignment into a grade in one step.

STUDY MODE

Study mode: Pomodoro and modes of your own

  • A study timer with modes of your own: a countdown, or Pomodoro with rounds and breaks.
  • Link a session to a test or an assignment and see how much you have studied for it.
  • Study history for 7 days, a month or a semester, with a breakdown by subject.
  • A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

AI TIPS (optional)

Statistics and AI tips right on your iPhone

  • A short summary of your week and a hint on when to study, based on your grades, free periods and upcoming tests.
  • It runs entirely on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, and nothing is sent to a server. Off by default, and it needs iOS 26 and a supported iPhone.

And there is more

For university students

ECTS credits, final grades, the ECTS-weighted average and your own scholarship threshold, with the grades you still need to reach it.

Widget, Live Activity, Siri

The next lesson on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Ask Siri for your next lesson, today's homework or your average, or add a grade by voice.

Private by design

No account, no ads, and your data stays on your iPhone. The optional AI tips run entirely on the device with Apple Intelligence.
