Your timetable, grades and homework in one place

Kajet is a student planner for school and university. Your whole day at a glance, grades with averages, homework and tests with reminders, and a study timer with Pomodoro. No account, no ads.

Download on the App StoreSupportPrivacy policy

Your day at a glance

Rings for this week's average, assignments and tests, a timeline of today's lessons and a to-do list you check off with one tap.

A timetable that bends

Day and week views, A and B weeks, cancelled lessons, substitutions, room changes and after-school events with a reminder to leave.

Grades and averages

A simple or weighted average with your own categories, retakes and comments. Statistics show your trend, your best subject and the one pulling you down.

For university students

ECTS credits, final grades, the ECTS-weighted average and your own scholarship threshold, with the grades you still need to reach it.

Homework and tests

Grouped by today, tomorrow and this week, with reminders a day or three days ahead. Turn a finished test into a grade in one step.

Study mode

A timer with your own modes: a countdown or Pomodoro with rounds and breaks. Link a session to a test, and see your study history.

Widget, Live Activity, Siri

The next lesson on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Ask Siri for your next lesson, today's homework or your average, or add a grade by voice.

Private by design

No account, no ads, and your data stays on your iPhone. The optional AI tips run entirely on the device with Apple Intelligence.

Made with the Polish school and university system in mind (grades 1–6, the 2.0–5.0 university scale, ECTS), but usable anywhere. Available in English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Chinese. For iPhone, iOS 17 or later; the AI tips need iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence.
