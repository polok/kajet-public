Student planner for iPhone

Your timetable, grades and homework in one place

Kajet is a student planner for school and university. Your whole day at a glance, grades with averages, homework and tests with reminders, and a study timer with Pomodoro. No account, no ads.

Download on the App StoreSee what it does
  • No account
  • No ads
  • Your data stays on your iPhone

Everything for school and university, in one app

Plan your day

  • Timetable with A/B weeks

    Day and week views, rooms, times.

  • Lesson changes

    Cancelled, substituted or moved for one day.

  • After-school events

    Sport, music, tutoring, with a reminder to leave.

  • Widget and Live Activity

    Next lesson on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Grades and averages

  • Simple or weighted average

    Your own categories, comments and retakes.

  • Statistics

    Trend over time, best subject, what pulls you down.

  • ECTS for university

    Credits, final grades, weighted ECTS average.

  • Scholarship goal

    Set a threshold, see the grades you still need.

Homework and study

  • Homework and tests

    Today, tomorrow, this week, later. One tap to check off.

  • Reminders

    A day or three ahead, in the evening, or when you choose.

  • Study mode

    Countdown or Pomodoro, linked to a test.

  • Study history

    7 days, a month or a semester, by subject.

Around the app

  • AI tips

    Weekly summary and when to study. Only on your iPhone.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Ask for your next lesson or add a grade by voice.

  • Semesters and school years

    Start a new one by copying the last.

  • 13 languages

    Follows your iPhone's language.
