Common questions

Is my data safe?

What you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded; the app only sends anonymous statistics and crash reports. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does the app need an account?

No. Kajet has no sign-in and works fully offline.

How do I change the language?

Kajet follows your iPhone's language. To use it in another language, open Settings → Kajet → Language (on iOS 18 or later: Settings → Apps → Kajet → Language).

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

The data lives on the device, so it travels with an iPhone transfer or with an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

I don't get reminders.

Check that notifications for Kajet are on in Settings → Notifications → Kajet, and that the reminder time is still ahead of you.

The AI tips don't show up.

They are off by default (More → AI suggestions) and need an iPhone with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence. When Apple Intelligence does not support your language yet, the tips are written in English.

How do I start a new semester?

Open More → School years → Add semester. Kajet offers to copy subjects, the timetable and events from a previous semester; grades and homework stay where they were.
